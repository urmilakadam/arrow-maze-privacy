Privacy Policy for Arrow Maze

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Urmila Kadam ("we", "us") handles information in the Arrow Maze mobile game ("the App").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location ourselves.

The App saves your game progress on your own device: your level, stars, settings (sound, theme, arrow colour) and the current board. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, which you can choose to watch for extra hints or to continue a level. AdMob may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. This can be used to show ads and to measure and prevent fraud. We do not receive this data.

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or another region that requires it, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

3. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can change your ad consent choice in the App if a consent option is shown.
  • You can delete all saved game data by using the Reset button in the App or by uninstalling it.

4. Children's privacy

Arrow Maze is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we do not share it. Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.

7. Contact us
